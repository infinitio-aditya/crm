## 1. Introduction

Infinitio Software ("we," "our," or "us") provides **Infinitio CRM** ("Application" or "Service"), an enterprise Customer Relationship Management mobile application designed to help businesses manage leads, client relationships, sales opportunities, quotations, orders, and field operations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related backend services. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the Application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us

When you register, authenticate, or use Infinitio CRM, you may provide:
- **Account & Profile Information:** Full name, business email address, phone number, job title/role, company name, employee identifier, and login credentials.
- **CRM Business Data:** Customer records, lead details, contact directory information (names, emails, phone numbers, billing/delivery addresses), opportunity valuations, quotations, orders, line items, transaction amounts, and operational notes.
- **Communications:** Remarks, follow-up notes, task schedules, and customer inquiries entered into the system.

### B. Information Collected Automatically

When you interact with the Application, our systems may automatically log:
- **Device & Technical Information:** Mobile device model, operating system version (Android / iOS), unique device identifiers, and language preferences.
- **Log & Usage Data:** Access timestamps, API response statuses, error logs, and performance diagnostics to maintain application stability.
- **Network Information:** IP address and network connection state (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) used to secure session authentication and maintain network connectivity.

### C. Permissions Used by the Application

- **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Communicates securely with authorized backend servers via encrypted HTTPS (TLS).
- **Network State (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Detects online/offline connectivity to manage request caching and data synchronization.
- **External Communications (`tel:`, `mailto:`, WhatsApp schemes):** Facilitates initiating phone calls or drafting emails to CRM contacts via native device handlers upon your explicit user tap.

*Note: The Application does not access your personal device contacts, camera, or precise GPS location without your explicit consent.*

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes:
1. **Core Service Delivery:** To authenticate user access, authorize role-based permissions, process sales pipelines, generate quotations, manage customer records, and track order fulfillment.
2. **Account Administration:** To manage user profiles, assign tasks, and maintain company organizational hierarchies.
3. **Security & Fraud Prevention:** To verify authorized sessions, audit permission access, enforce security policies, and protect against unauthorized access.
4. **Service Reliability:** To diagnose crashes, monitor server response times, and improve mobile user experience.
5. **Customer Support:** To investigate and resolve technical queries and support requests submitted by authorized users.

---

## 4. How Information is Shared and Disclosed

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party data brokers or advertisers. Data is shared only under the following strictly governed circumstances:
- **Within Your Organization:** Business CRM records (leads, accounts, orders) are shared among authorized personnel within your enterprise tenant based on configured role-based access controls (RBAC).
- **Service Providers:** Authorized cloud infrastructure, hosting, and database providers (e.g., cloud server providers) that process data under strict data protection agreements.
- **Legal Compliance:** When required by applicable law, regulation, subpoena, or lawful governmental authority request.
- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, subject to continued adherence to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All client-server communication is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3).
- **Secure Local Storage:** Authentication tokens and sensitive session parameters on your device are stored using encrypted device storage (`flutter_secure_storage` utilizing Android Keystore and iOS Keychain).
- **Role-Based Access Control:** Fine-grained authorization controls restrict access to customer and financial data strictly to authorized enterprise users.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain personal and CRM data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services and fulfill the obligations outlined in enterprise agreements:
- **Active Accounts:** Data is retained while your user account and organizational subscription remain active.
- **Account & Data Deletion:** Users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time. Detailed instructions are available in our [Data Deletion Policy](DATA_DELETION.md) or by emailing **privacy@infinitiosoftware.com**.
- **Statutory Retention:** Certain transaction logs and financial records (e.g., invoiced orders, tax receipts) may be retained for statutory periods required by applicable taxation and accounting regulations.
