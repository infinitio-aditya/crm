# Account and Data Deletion Policy

**App Name:** Infinitio CRM  
**Package Name / Application ID:** `com.infinitio.crm_app`  
**Developer / Company:** Infinitio Software  
**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026  
**Support Email:** contact@infinitiosoftware.com  

---

## 1. Overview

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1.1(v)), **Infinitio CRM** provides users with clear, transparent, and direct mechanisms to request the deletion of their user account and associated personal data.

Users may request deletion:
1. **Directly within the mobile application** (In-App), or
2. **Via our web-based request channel** (without needing to install or re-install the mobile app).

---

## 2. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

### Method A: In-App Deletion Request

If you have access to the Infinitio CRM mobile application on your device:
1. Open **Infinitio CRM** and sign in to your account.
2. Tap the **Navigation Menu / Profile Avatar** and select **Settings**.
3. Scroll to the **Account & Security** section.
4. Select **Delete Account / Data Deletion**.
5. Read the confirmation summary detailing data removal and tap **Confirm Account Deletion**.
6. Enter your password or confirmation token to authorize the request.

Your session will be terminated immediately, and an automated deletion workflow will be initiated.

---

### Method B: Web-Based / Email Deletion Request (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your mobile device:
1. Send an email to **privacy@infinitiosoftware.com** or **support@infinitiosoftware.com** with the subject line:  
   `Account Deletion Request - Infinitio CRM`.
2. Include the following details in your message:
   - **Registered Business Email Address** associated with your Infinitio CRM account.
   - **Full Name** and **Organization / Company Name**.
   - Specific scope requested (e.g., "Delete my user account and personal profile data" or "Full tenant data erasure").
3. Our Data Protection Team will verify your identity within **48 business hours** by sending a verification link to your registered email address.
4. Upon verification, your account and personal data will be scheduled for permanent purge.

---

## 3. Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon receipt and verification of a valid deletion request, the following information will be permanently removed from our active production databases within **30 days**:

| Data Category | Specific Elements Deleted |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Authentication & Profile** | Login credentials, passwords (hashed), session tokens, user profile name, email, phone number, and employee identifier. |
| **User Settings & Preferences** | Theme settings, UI preferences, language selections, and notification configs. |
| **Personal Notes & Tasks** | Personal task assignments, remarks, scheduled reminders, and activity timeline entries created by the user. |
| **Device & Log Data** | Push notification tokens, temporary cache entries, and user-specific IP session mappings. |

---

## 4. Data That May Be Retained and Justification

Certain categories of information may not be immediately deleted or may be retained in anonymized/aggregated form where required by law, enterprise contracts, or legitimate compliance obligations:

1. **Invoiced Orders & Financial Records:**
   - *Data Retained:* Order numbers, invoice totals, tax records (GST/VAT receipts), line items, and payment transaction references.
   - *Justification:* Retained strictly to comply with statutory taxation, accounting audits, and commercial bookkeeping laws (typically retained for 7 years as mandated by financial regulatory authorities).
2. **Security & Fraud Prevention Logs:**
   - *Data Retained:* Anonymized server security access logs and audit trails of critical system events.
   - *Retention Period:* Up to 90 days, after which they are automatically rotated and permanently purged.
3. **Enterprise Organization Data:**
   - If your account was provisioned under an enterprise company subscription, CRM records created on behalf of the organization (e.g., company-owned customer accounts and opportunities) may remain part of the enterprise tenant database as property of the subscribing organization, while your personal identifying user profile is detached and deleted.

---

## 5. Deletion Timeline

- **Immediate Action (Day 0):** User account is disabled. Active authentication tokens are revoked; the user cannot sign in.
- **Verification & Processing (Days 1–7):** Identity confirmed, and data marked for deletion across distributed storage.
- **Permanent Purge (Days 1–30):** Personal records are permanently wiped from active production databases and background cache.
- **Backup Deletion (Up to 60 Days):** Encrypted backup snapshots rotate and overwrite residual data within standard backup lifecycle retention cycles.
